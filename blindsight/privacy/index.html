2. In short

Blindsight does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

3. No data collection

Blindsight is a card game that is played fully offline. The app contains no networking code: it makes no internet connections, transmits no data to us or to third parties, and fetches no content from the internet. There are no ads, no analytics or tracking tools, no user accounts, and no in-app purchases.

4. Local settings

The app stores your preferences — such as sound, haptics, appearance, language, and game progress (for example, whether the tutorial has been seen or which opponent levels are unlocked) — exclusively on your device. This data never leaves your device, is not visible to us, and is removed when you delete the app. There is no iCloud synchronization.

5. Apple App Store

The app is distributed through the Apple App Store. In that context, Apple processes data under its own responsibility, for example for purchase and download, crash reports, or — if you have opted in with Apple — anonymized usage statistics. We have no influence over this; Apple's privacy policy applies: apple.com/legal/privacy. At most, we receive aggregated statistics from Apple that cannot be traced back to individual persons.

6. Children

Since the app collects no data, no data is collected from children either.

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to information, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, as well as the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority. Since we do not store any personal data about you, requests concerning data from the App Store purchase are generally directed to Apple. You are welcome to contact us at the e-mail address above at any time.

8. Changes

If the app's functionality changes — for example through an online mode in a later version — this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will be revised.
