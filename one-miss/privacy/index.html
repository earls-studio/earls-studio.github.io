Privacy Policy for "One Miss"

Last updated: November 28, 2026 (version 1.2). One Miss was previously called "Blink Grid".

1. Controller

Lucas Hesseldieck, Amalienstraße 41, 80799 Munich, Germany

Email: support@earlsstudio.com

2. The short version

One Miss stores your game progress on your device. We run no servers of our own and never see your personal data. The app shows ads through Google AdMob. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, the app first asks for your consent. Only if you agree there does Apple's tracking prompt appear. Ads are personalised only if you say yes to both. A reward from an ad never depends on your answers.

3. Data stored on your device

Game progress, records, coins, purchased content and settings are stored locally on your device. Deleting the app removes this data (see section 8 for the optional iCloud backup).

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (Google Ireland Ltd., Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland) to show rewarded ads and interstitial ads. A rewarded ad only appears after you tap a button for it, for example to revive after a miss. Purchasing any in-app product permanently removes interstitial ads.

To deliver, measure and secure ads, Google processes the following data:

  • Device identifiers, including the advertising identifier (IDFA) only if you allow tracking (section 6). Google may use them to track you across other companies' apps and websites.
  • Advertising data (e.g. which ads were shown and tapped) and product interaction data.
  • Approximate location, derived from your IP address.
  • Diagnostic data: crash data, performance data and other diagnostic data.

Device identifiers, advertising data, product interaction and approximate location may be linked to your identity. Diagnostic data is not. Ad attribution also uses Apple's SKAdNetwork framework.

Details on Google's processing: https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://support.google.com/admob/answer/9012903

5. Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland)

At the first launch, the app shows a consent message from Google (User Messaging Platform) with the choices "Consent", "Do not consent" and "Manage options". The legal basis for the processing described in section 4 is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR).

If you do not consent, the app does not show Apple's tracking prompt and never requests personalised ads. Google decides from your choices whether it can still show an ad in a limited form without personalisation. If no ad is available, the button for it is hidden. That happens to every player from time to time and is not a consequence of your choice.

You can change or withdraw your choice at any time via Settings → Ad privacy options in the app.

Outside these regions, no consent message and no tracking prompt appear, the advertising identifier is never requested, and only non-personalised ads are requested. Processing there is based on our legitimate interest in financing a free app (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

6. Apple's tracking prompt (App Tracking Transparency)

Only if you consented in the message from section 5, Apple's prompt asks whether One Miss may track you across other companies' apps and websites. If you allow it, Google receives the advertising identifier and may show personalised ads. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", the advertising identifier is not shared, and ads are requested as non-personalised.

The game, all bonuses and every reward work exactly the same whatever you answer. You can change your choice at any time in iOS under Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

7. Game Center (optional)

You can voluntarily enable global leaderboards in the settings. Your scores are then submitted via Apple Game Center; Apple processes your Game Center nickname and scores under its own responsibility. Details: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/. The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you can withdraw at any time using the switch in the settings.

8. iCloud backup

Records and lifetime counters are mirrored to your personal iCloud key-value storage so they survive reinstalling the app. This data lives in your Apple account; we cannot access it. You can disable iCloud for One Miss in the iOS settings.

9. In-app purchases

Purchases are handled entirely by the Apple App Store. We never receive payment or account details. Apple only shares anonymised sales figures with us.

10. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, as well as the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. For data processed by Google or Apple under their own responsibility, please also contact these companies directly; we are happy to help at the email address above.

11. Changes

If the app's functionality changes, this policy will be updated. The current version can always be found at this address.
