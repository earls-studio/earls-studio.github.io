2. In short

Tiny Turf itself does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. There is no tracking, no analytics, no server of our own and no account with us.

3. Data stored on your device

Your game progress (coins, trophies, league, figures bought, daily tasks, world map) and your preferences (for example sound, haptics, colour assist, language, whether the rules have been shown) are stored exclusively on your device. This data never leaves your device, is not visible to us, and is removed when you delete the app. There is no iCloud synchronization.

4. Game Center (optional)

You can switch on Game Center in the settings. Only then does the app report your trophies, the land of the current week and your progress on the achievements to Apple Game Center, so that they appear in leaderboards and achievements. Apple processes your Game Center name and these values; the controller for this processing is Apple Inc. or Apple Distribution International Ltd. Details: apple.com/legal/privacy.

The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). You can switch it off again at any time; from then on the app reports nothing. Without switching it on, nothing is transmitted.

5. In-app purchases

Coin packs and the starter pack are handled entirely by the Apple App Store. We receive no payment or account data. On your device the app only checks that Apple has confirmed a purchase and adds it to your local game progress. Apple only shares aggregated sales figures with us. Apple's privacy policy applies.

6. Apple App Store

The app is distributed through the Apple App Store. In that context, Apple processes data under its own responsibility, for example for purchase and download, crash reports, or — if you have opted in with Apple — anonymized usage statistics. We have no influence over this. At most, we receive aggregated statistics from Apple that cannot be traced back to individual persons.

7. No sharing, no third parties

The app includes no third-party services (no ad networks, no analytics tools, no social media integrations) and shares no data.

8. Children

Since the app itself collects no data, no data is collected from children either.

9. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to information, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, as well as the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority. Since we do not store any personal data about you, requests concerning Game Center and purchase data are generally directed to Apple. You are welcome to contact us at the e-mail address above at any time.

10. Changes

If the app's functionality changes — for example through advertising in a later version — this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will be revised.
