2. In short

  • Tiny Turf has no server of its own and no account. We ourselves store no data about you.
  • The app shows ads only when you start them yourself: with a button that gives a bonus in return (for example double coins). No ad ever appears by itself.
  • The ads are delivered by Google AdMob. In doing so, Google processes data from your device (section 6).
  • You always get your bonus for watching the ad, whatever you answer to the questions about consent and tracking.
  • Your game progress is kept on your device and, in addition, in your own iCloud (section 4).

3. Data stored on your device

Your game progress (coins, trophies, league, figures, tasks, world map, purchases counted) and your preferences (for example music, sound, haptics, colour assist, language, whether the rules have been shown) are stored on your device. They are not visible to us.

4. Game progress in your iCloud

So that your progress survives a new iPhone or a reinstall, the app keeps a copy of it in your own iCloud (Apple's storage for app data). The copy contains the game progress only, no name and no contact details. We have no access to it; it is processed by Apple within your iCloud account. Without iCloud on the device no copy is made. You can switch iCloud off for the app in the iOS settings. The legal basis is providing the feature you use with the app (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

5. Game Center (optional)

You can switch on Game Center in the settings. Only then does the app report your trophies, the land of the current week and your progress on the achievements to Apple Game Center, so that they appear in leaderboards and achievements. Apple processes your Game Center name and these values; the controller for this processing is Apple Inc. or Apple Distribution International Ltd. Details: apple.com/legal/privacy.

The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). You can switch it off again at any time; from then on the app reports nothing.

6. Advertising (Google AdMob)

When ads appear: only after you tap a button that offers an ad in return for a bonus. There are no ads between rounds and no banners.

Who delivers them: Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland (“Google”), through its AdMob service. For this purpose the app contains Google's ad library (Google Mobile Ads SDK) and Google's consent library (User Messaging Platform).

What data Google processes (according to Google's own disclosures for the library):

  • device identifiers, including the device's advertising identifier (IDFA) – the latter only if you allowed tracking in Apple's prompt;
  • the IP address, from which Google derives an approximate location;
  • which ads were shown and whether they were tapped;
  • performance, crash and diagnostic data of the ad library.

Google uses this data to deliver ads, to measure them and to prevent abuse, and – only with your consent – to personalise ads. Google and its advertising partners are responsible for this processing themselves. Details: policies.google.com/privacy and How Google uses data from apps.

Your choice

  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the first time you tap an ad button the app asks, through Google's consent form, whether information may be stored on or read from your device and used for personalised advertising. You can consent, decline or choose individual purposes.
  • Only if you consented there does Apple then ask whether the app may track you across other companies' apps and websites (App Tracking Transparency).
  • Only if you agree to both does the app ask Google for personalised ads. In every other case it explicitly asks for non-personalised ads.
  • Outside these countries no prompt appears; there the app always asks for non-personalised ads and does not ask to track.
  • If you decline, you still get ads and the bonus.

Legal basis for storing and reading information on your device and for personalised advertising is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, Section 25(1) of the German TDDDG). For delivering non-personalised ads that you requested yourself it is our legitimate interest in financing the free game (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Withdrawing consent: where Google requires consent, the app's settings contain the row “Change ad consent”. You can switch tracking off in the iOS settings under “Privacy & Security → Tracking”. Withdrawal applies for the future.

Transfers outside the EU: Google may also process data in the United States. Google LLC is certified under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework; otherwise Google relies on standard contractual clauses.

7. In-app purchases

Coin packs, the starter pack and “Super Clay” are handled entirely by the Apple App Store. We receive no payment or account data. On your device the app only checks that Apple has confirmed a purchase and adds it to your game progress. Apple only shares aggregated sales figures with us. Apple's privacy policy applies.

8. Apple App Store

The app is distributed through the Apple App Store. In that context, Apple processes data under its own responsibility, for example for purchase and download, crash reports, or — if you have opted in with Apple — anonymized usage statistics. We have no influence over this. At most, we receive aggregated statistics from Apple that cannot be traced back to individual persons.

9. No other third parties

Apart from Google's libraries for ads and consent, the app includes no third-party services: no analytics tools, no social media integrations. We ourselves share no data.

10. Children

The app is not specifically directed at children. We ourselves collect no data; section 6 applies to advertising.

11. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to information, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, the right to withdraw consent at any time, and the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority. Since we do not store any personal data about you, requests concerning advertising data are generally directed to Google, and requests concerning Game Center, iCloud and purchases to Apple. You are welcome to contact us at the e-mail address above at any time.

12. Changes

If the app's functionality changes, this policy will be updated before the version concerned is released, and the date above will be revised.
